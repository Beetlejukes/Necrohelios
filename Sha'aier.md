# Overview
Ancient beings from [[The Time Before Time]] whos charge was the maintenance of perfect balance within the universe. This charge was eventually failed, and the Sha'aier died off one by one, their bodies fracturing and becoming the material which re-formed the universe into the one in which we now live. 

While there were countless of these beings in existence during their time, only a select few have been retroactively given names by the very few living beings which knew or know of their existence. Strangely, despite the Sha'aier themselves never having had use for language and, therefore, never having named themselves, every race which deigns to name them arrives at the same names despite their historical inability to contact the other races.

Despite their staggering similarities, each member of the Sha'aier contained it's own unique makeup and, therefore, it's own unique drive and it's own unique aftermath. For instance, while [[#Grel]] and [[#Heth]] were as similar to one another as all Sha'aier, one held within it a stronger drive towards the expansion of life and the chaos it brings while the other held within it a stronger drive towards a sterile equilibrium and the stability it brings.
