# Rough Synopsis

You are a maintenance engineer returning from a deep-crust subterranean research expedition on the exoplanet [[Chiros II]] under contract with the [[Galfoil Industrial Concern]]. Unbeknownst to yourself and the crew you are returning alongside, the planet you are returning to was only days ago the site of the [[First Shock-Harvest of the Frontier Age]]. Near the end of your two-week voyage beneath the remarkably thick crust of [[Chiros II]], the [[Necrohelion]] appeared out of [[Slipspace]] near the planet and almost immediately beset it; survivors of the attack are few and far between, and the landscape shows far fewer signs of struggle than do the populated research center and nearby supporting communities.

As you near the surface and find that you cannot successfully hail the research center, your crew mates begin postulating and guessing among one another as to why communications could have gone offline; as you check the systems, everything seems to be in working order. Your crew leave the craft in order to make direct contact with workers at the research center as you stay behind briefly to finish re-assembling post-diagnosis.

After finishing the re-assembly, you gather your light personal inventory (perhaps a protein bar and some tools) and disembark to meet up with your crew mates and get some r&r. When you do so, however, you are met with a disturbing and terrifying sight: the facility has been overwhelmed and ransacked, and the only living soul in sight is one of your crew mates slumped over against the craft and weakly clutching a gaping, almost entirely cauterized wound in his chest. His last words will be ones of warning, informing you that a strange, lanky humanoid shot him and incapacitated and kidnapped the others.

From this point, you will spend some time exploring [[Chiros II Research Facility Gamma]] and gathering both equipment with which to defend yourself in combat and with knowledge as to what has occurred here and how you can find your way to safety; that way to safety will be an expensive private spacecraft which you will discover is tucked away in an auxiliary landing bay near the executive offices on the outskirts of the facility. This private spacecraft will be assigned co-ordinates, found elsewhere along the journey, to a nearby [[Galfoil Industrial Concern]] [[System Hub Station]] which the player will depart for as the story wraps up.

# Settings

#### Surfacing
- The player will be introduced to their character, their crew mates, and briefly to the universe which they will be inhabiting and exploring.
	*What is the environment like?*
	- The interior of the [[Galfoil Deep-Crust Survey and Analysis Platform]] *(or 'D-SAP')* is sparsely decorated but surprisingly well-designed on multiple fronts. The player will have spent ample time aboard them in the past, given their role as maintenance engineer.
