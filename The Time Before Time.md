# Overview

More of a theoretical chronological denomination, the 'Time Before Time' is the name most widely used by the [[Vampyr]] archivists to describe the age before the death of the [[Sha'aier]] and the formation of the galaxy out of their bodies. No living being has true memory of this era aside from the [[Sire of Lore]], but even this memory is an implant and cannot be accurately described to another living being.

During this era, the [[Sha'aier]] roamed the space between spaces and collectively maintained the balance between expansion and retraction, between life and death. This balance was eventually broken, and the ancient beings began to die off one by one, each one in it's time peacably fracturing into the galaxies which occupy the universe now. Each of these galaxies was formed with the entirety of each of these beings; some of them had a stronger penchant for creation than for destruction, such as [[Sha'aier#Heth|Heth]] whos body formed our galaxy and the [[Humans]] which inhabit it, while others were their inverse such as [[Sha'aier#Grel|Grel]] whos body formed the home galaxy of the [[Vampyr]].
