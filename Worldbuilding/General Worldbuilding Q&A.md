## Design Pillars

#### ***Q:*** What ideas and themes should guide this project?

***A:***
- *The destructiveness of ambition devoid of wisdom*(humankind attempting at great and terrible cost to utilize ancient alien technology)
- *The perseverence and power of the human spirit* (humankind refusing to lose itself in the face of seemingly insurmountable and unending struggle)
- *The cold and uncaring embrace of the universe* (this universe was not made for us alone, we simply inhabit it; we were not the first ones here, and we will not be the last)
- *Decisiveness under great duress and the pain it causes* (exploring how priorities and morals can shift or shatter under terrible circumstances, and the often unhealthy methods we find to cope with our decisions)

#### ***Q:*** What are the fundamental material/metaphysical truths of this world which inform the courses of it's history?

***A:***
- *Our galaxy is the shattered, decaying corpse of an ancient God*
	- While there now exist vanishingly few beings who carry this knowledge, it informs the material basis of the world and the color and form taken by the magic within it.
	- During [[The Time Before Time]] there existed a group of entities which have been known as Gods, or collectively as God, by countless [[Humans|Human]] civilizations. To the archivists among the [[Vampyr]], however, they are called [[Sha'aier]].

***A:***
- *Magic exists in this world, though it is difficult to channel and even harder to control without very advanced technological assistance*
	- While [[Magic|magic]] emanates throughout the galaxy akin to solar radiation, it is far too tempermental, intermittent, and unstable in it's "wild" state to be useful to human users. Because of this, nearly every human society has discovered technological methods of capturing, storing, channeling, and, often, concentrating/refining magical energies.
	- The actual nucleation point for this magical energy is the [[Preterbiological Degradation]] of the [[Sha'aier]] which died to form our galaxy; while many elements of the physical body of the entity formed the physical materiele of our galaxy, it's metaphysical body broke apart to form the [[Stars]] which light our galaxy and, unbeknownst to humanity, color our base psychology.
