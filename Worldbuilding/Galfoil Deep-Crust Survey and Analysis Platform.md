#Vehicle-Industrial #Vehicle-Research 

## Overview

The Galfoil Deep-Crust Survey and Analysis Platform, or 'D-SAP' as it's colloquially known, is a heavy industrial subterranean research platform designed and manufactured by [[Galfoil Industrial Concern]]. It's intended uses include deep-crust penetration and navigation, survey and research of terrestrial materials, extraction of materials in testing quantities, and sustenance of a crew of up to 12 in relative comfort for up to four-week intervals.
